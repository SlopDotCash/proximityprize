# LS18

## At A Glance

`LS18` is the Lyubashevsky–Seiler EUROCRYPT paper showing that, for a prime `q ≡ 5 (mod 8)` and
power-of-two degree, nonzero ring elements with small norm are invertible in
`Z_q[X]/(X^d + 1)`. This is what makes the relaxation factors in the weak-binding extractor
genuinely invertible.

## What ArkLib Uses From This Paper

- Corollary 1.2 (informally: short elements, of centered Euclidean norm `< √q`, are units),
  formalized as `isUnit_of_l1Norm_le` in the `ℓ₁` regime below.

## Formal Statement And Status

- `isUnit_of_l1Norm_le` is **proved** (no `sorry`, no hypothesis wrapper). Its exact formal
  regime: over `powTwoCyclotomic α` (`X^{2^α} + 1`) with a prime `q ≡ 5 (mod 8)`, any `c` with
  centered `ℓ₁` norm `0 < ‖c‖₁ ≤ κ` and `κ² < q` is a unit. This `ℓ₁` hypothesis is a
  sufficient condition (it gives `‖c‖₂² ≤ ‖c‖₁² < q`); the Euclidean-norm form of the informal
  corollary is not the statement of this theorem.
- The proof is an elementary `mod q` divisibility count (the `k = 2` splitting case), with
  supporting lemmas in `NormBounds/LsCore.lean`; it does not use ideal-lattice minimum-distance
  machinery.

## Open Formalization Gaps

- `isUnit_of_l1Norm_le` is proved, including the algebraic non-unit divisibility core and the
  norm argument used by Hachi.
- No open gap remains for the specific short-invertibility result ArkLib currently imports from
  `LS18`.
- A Euclidean-norm-hypothesis variant matching the informal corollary verbatim is not stated in
  ArkLib; the proved statement uses the `ℓ₁` hypothesis above.
