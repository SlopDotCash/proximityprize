# δ* #466 — dyadic tower split and cancellation profile (2026-07-08)

## Hypothesis

R176 suggested a polarization/compensation law.  R178 tests whether that law is
generated by the dyadic tower

```text
μ_n ⊂ μ_{2n}.
```

A `μ_{2n}` period is the sum of two `μ_n` child periods, so stable
polarization might come from a stable cancellation/splitting recurrence.

Probe: `scripts/probes/probe_r178_dyadic_tower_split.py`.

## Result

Exact same-prime tower comparisons:

```text
p          n->2n   childS1 parentS1 childLow parentLow childS4 parentS4 splitRatio[min,mean,max] cancel[min,mean] polar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1048609    16 ->32  0.325   0.322    0.512    0.515     0.043   0.046    [0.000,0.498,1.000] [0.000,0.634] 0.634
16777601   32 ->64  0.321   0.319    0.516    0.519     0.045   0.045    [0.000,0.500,1.000] [0.000,0.636] 0.635
268437889  64 ->128 0.319   0.318    0.518    0.519     0.045   0.045    [0.000,0.500,1.000] [0.000,0.636] 0.636
```

Definitions:

* `childS1`, `parentS1`: fraction with `X ≥ 1`.
* `childLow`, `parentLow`: fraction with `X < 0.5`.
* `childS4`, `parentS4`: fraction with `X ≥ 4`.
* `splitRatio`: normalized parent squared magnitude divided by the sum of the two child normalized
  squared magnitudes.
* `cancel`: `|a+b|²/(|a|+|b|)²` for the two child periods.
* `polar`: relative imbalance of the two child squared magnitudes.

## Verdict

The dyadic bulk/tail distribution is nearly invariant under doubling
`n → 2n`.  The period split has a stable cancellation profile:

```text
mean split ratio ≈ 0.5
mean phase-cancellation factor ≈ 0.636
mean child-pair polarization ≈ 0.635
```

This is a strong structural proof clue.  The compensation law may be provable
by induction along the dyadic tower: show that the two-child period split
preserves a bin-budget/MGF invariant.

New proof target:

```text
Find a dyadic tower invariant I on the normalized coset spectrum such that
  I(μ_n) ≤ 2  implies  I(μ_{2n}) ≤ 2,
where I can be instantiated as the R168/R177 bin-budget or MGF certificate.
```

This is more structural than treating each `n` independently with a raw
survival-count estimate.
