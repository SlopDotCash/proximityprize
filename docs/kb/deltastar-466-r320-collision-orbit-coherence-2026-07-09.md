# R320 Collision-Orbit Coherence and Primitive Recurrences

Date: 2026-07-09
Issue: #466 / Proximity Prize

## Probe

`probe_r320_collision_orbit_coherence.py` builds the exact shadow histogram,
pushes it to the field, and groups ordered off-diagonal collision mass by
signed cyclic-shift orbits of the difference relation.

Two hostile cells were compared:

1. the depth-3 `c=3` exact-Wick violator `p=21523361`;
2. the worst centered depth-4 generic cell `p=1439393` from R319.

## Result

The `c=3` cell is maximally coherent: 96.7% of its mass is generated by the
binomial relation orbit `3 + zeta^5 = 0`.

Surprisingly, the generic depth-4 cell is also coherent.  It has only five
relation orbits, and the first two carry 96.4% of the collision mass.  The
dominant relations are, up to sign and shift,

```text
f = 2 + x + x^4 + x^8 + x^12,
h = (1+x)x^4 - x - 3.
```

They are not independent.  Modulo `x^16+1`,

```text
(x^4 - 1) f = h.
```

Since `x^4-1` has only dyadic norm, it is invertible at the odd prime.  Thus
the two orbits are the same primitive recurrence class under a cyclotomic-unit
multiplier.  The resultant confirms the field:

```text
Res(x^16+1, h) = 2^5 * 1439393.
```

So 96.4% of the worst depth-4 collision mass is generated by one primitive
small-height recurrence class, despite the prime being arithmetically generic.

## Revised proof target

The rational-binomial inverse is false, but a more general recurrence-class
dichotomy survives both hostile examples:

```text
collision mass
  = sum over primitive recurrence classes modulo cyclotomic-unit multipliers;

each class either has small total mass,
or is generated by a bounded-height recurrence whose transfer operator has
Gaussian moment growth <= K^r (2r-1)!! n^r.
```

The new technical target is to define a canonical primitive class and prove a
uniform transfer bound depending exponentially on recurrence height, not on
`n` or `p`.  At prize depth, polynomially many classes can be absorbed into
the same exponential constant.
