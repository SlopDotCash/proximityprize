# R321--R322 Primitive Recurrence Saturation

Date: 2026-07-09
Issue: #466 / Proximity Prize

## Discovery

R320 showed that the worst generic depth-4 cell has most collision mass in one
primitive recurrence class.  R321 tests the same phenomenon on every one of
the 92 in-window `n=32` K-bad primes.

For each prime, let `f` be the signed cyclic-shift orbit carrying the most
off-diagonal shadow collision mass.  The complete census finds

```text
|Res(x^16+1,f)| / p = 2  in 76 cells,
|Res(x^16+1,f)| / p = 4  in 15 cells,
|Res(x^16+1,f)| / p = 8  in  1 cell.
```

There are zero odd cofactors.  The top recurrence orbit carries between 71.7%
and 100% of collision mass, with mean 95.6%.

## Lattice consequence

Multiplication by `f` on `Z[x]/(x^16+1)` has image lattice of index
`|Res(x^16+1,f)|`.  The evaluation kernel has index `p`.  Hence their quotient
has order `2`, `4`, or `8`.  The Lean file
`_R321DyadicSaturationBridge.lean` proves the abstract consequence:

```text
if card(kernel / recurrenceLattice) divides 8,
then 8 * kernel is contained in recurrenceLattice.
```

Thus, subject only to the standard determinant/resultant identification, every
modular relation in all 92 cells is generated by one short recurrence after at
most eightfold dyadic scaling.

The same exact pattern occurs for the R318 `c=3` violator:

```text
Res(x^16+1, 3+x^5) = 2 * 21523361.
```

## Deep-moment falsification

R322 evaluates all Gauss-period cosets at every depth through `ceil(log p)`.
Across all 92 saturated depth-4 cells:

```text
max K_eff over all cells/depths = 0.9999922 at r=1,
max K_eff at logarithmic depth  = 0.9209684.
```

Every cell is strictly real-Wick-subgaussian at every checked depth.  As a
small-field adversarial control, the same complete calculation was run on all
57 primes in the `n=16` depth-3 bad-prime census.  Its global maximum is also
the Parseval rung (`K_eff=0.9996147`); only one cell peaks at r=2, and the worst
log-depth constant is `0.7673297`.

The exceptional `c=3`, `n=32` cell remains the known shallow super-Wick case,
but R318 bounds its peak by `K_eff=1.0138` and shows turnover before log depth.

## Proposed theorem

The evidence now supports a stronger structural target:

```text
PrimitiveSaturationDichotomy:
  either the centered collision mass is already K^r-Wick,
  or there is a short primitive recurrence f with
    |Res(Phi_n,f)| = 2^a p,  a = O(r),
  whose recurrence lattice contains 2^a times the full evaluation kernel.
```

The quotient contributes only `2^a = exp(O(r))`, compatible with the allowed
`K^r` loss.  The remaining hard step is a uniform return-probability bound for
the principal recurrence lattice.  This is not supplied by saturation alone:
after Fourier duality it can collapse back to the Paley spectrum.  Any proof
must use the short/banded nature of `f`, for example through a sparse
trigonometric-polynomial sublevel bound or a direct recurrence rewrite count.
