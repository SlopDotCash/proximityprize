`Research/ProximityPrize/Frontier/REnergyCyclicFloorAllDepth.lean` proves
`rEnergy_ge_cyclic_floor_all_depth`: E_r >= 3 N^r - 2 N^(r-2) for r >= 3,

of size N. `swap_floor_lt_cyclic_floor_all_depth_of_two_le` proves strict
improvement over 2 N^r - N^(r-1) for N >= 2. This is an unconditional lower
collision count, not the missing upper bound on character sums.

to a temporary output directory against cached substrate imports. All passed;

## Di Benedetto: no unconditional beat established

[Daniel di Benedetto et al., Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2003.06165v1#S3)
in *New estimates for exponential sums over multiplicative subgroups and
intervals in prime fields* gives H^(2689/2880) p^(1/72), up to subpower losses,
for p^(1/4) < H < p^(1/2). Replacing the p factor using p < H^4 gives the leading
exponent 2689/2880 + 1/18 = 2849/2880, approximately 0.98924.

The proposed stronger-energy recalibration uses H^(65/72) p^(1/72), hence
65/72 + 1/18 = 23/24, approximately 0.95833. Add the p factor exactly once.
This arithmetic comparison is proved in
`Research/ProximityPrize/Frontier/_AvCR_DiBenedettoWired.lean`.

Its `DiBenedettoThm31Sidon` structure includes the specialized analytic conclusion
as a hypothesis. `sidon_corollary_of_thm31` consumes that structure; it does not
prove the specialized estimate from the energy bounds alone. Uniform transfer
of the required energy bounds to the prize fields also remains open. Therefore
neither the smaller number nor an axiom-clean conditional consumer establishes
an unconditional improvement on the published theorem. Constants and subpower
losses also prevent reading these exponents as finite numerical guarantees.

Older notes with “CONFIRMED” or “VERDICT” in their filenames are not sufficient
evidence of an unconditional beat. The June 15 `dibenedetto-beat-FINAL-status`
note and the current theorem hypotheses qualify those historical claims.
Interpolation radii concern a different quantity and cannot establish this beat.

## Visual example

The message polynomial is 1 + 2x + 3x^2 + x^3 over F17. Its codeword on
[1,2,4,8,16,15,13,9] is [7,8,2,7,1,1,11,5]. For slider value t, add 5 modulo 17
to the first t symbols, then count all codewords at Hamming distance <= t.
The exact counts for t = 0,1,2,3,4 are 1,1,1,4,54. An independent Python
enumeration using polynomial powers reproduced the Horner-based TypeScript
implementation. The build tests every slider value.
