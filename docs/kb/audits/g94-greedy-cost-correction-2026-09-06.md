# G94 greedy chaining interpretation

The G94 metric probe constructs one sequence of greedy admissible nets and evaluates its weighted distance cost. This is a candidate cost (an upper bound for the infimum over admissible nets), not the smallest possible chaining functional. Rescaling by the measured domination factor only chooses the least legal scale for that fixed metric; it does not optimize the nets or metric.

The introductory description now makes that distinction. An AST comparison after removing docstrings and print expressions confirms that the numerical computation is unchanged.
