## 1. The dictionary (exact, proven, probe-checked): the sections ARE Hecke points

> `L_S(p) := ker(Z^S → F_p, a ↦ Σ_i a_i h^{s_i})`, with `c_S := (h^{s_1}, …, h^{s_d})`.

**Proposition 1 (index and dual — two-line proofs).**
(i) `L_S(p)` has index exactly `p` in `Z^d` (the defining map is surjective since every `h^{s_i}`
is a unit). (ii) The dual lattice is exactly
> `L_S(p)^* = Z^d + Z·(c_S/p)`.
*Proof of (ii):* `⟨c_S/p, a⟩ = (a·c_S)/p ∈ Z` for `a ∈ L_S` by definition, so RHS ⊆ L*; the RHS
contains `Z^d` with quotient generated by `c_S/p`, of order exactly `p` in `(R/Z)^d` (a unit
entry), so covol(RHS) = 1/p = covol(L*); equal covolume + containment ⟹ equality. ∎
(Probe part (1): 1000/1000 membership and integrality spot-checks at n = 16, p = 65537/65617.)

**Proposition 2 (Hecke-point identification).** The index-`p` sublattices of `Z^d` are exactly
the kernels `ker(a ↦ a·c mod p)` for `[c] ∈ P^{d−1}(F_p)`; rescaled by `p^{−1/d}` they form the
standard minuscule Hecke orbit `T_p·[Z^d] ⊂ X_d = SL_d(Z)\SL_d(R)`, `deg T_p = #P^{d−1}(F_p) =
(p^d−1)/(p−1) ≈ p^{d−1}`. Hence
> `x_S(p) := p^{−1/d}·L_S(p)` **is the Hecke point of `T_p[Z^d]` with parameter
> `[c_S] = [h^{s_1} : … : h^{s_d}] ∈ P^{d−1}(F_p)`.**
This completes the SST reformulation (essay §2.3, round-2-clarified) to the group level — the

**Proposition 3 (dilation = projectivization).** The `μ_n`-dilation `b ↦ ub` scales `c_S` by a

The multiplier `S ↦ kS` (`k` odd) satisfies the **exact Galois-twist identity**

## 5. Direction III (×2-rigidity / BLMV): structurally inapplicable, and the rank-1 statement IS BGK

The seed: "μ_n = orbit of the doubling map; the empirical phase measure at scale 1/p as an
almost-×2-invariant measure; effective rigidity gives entropy lower bounds."

**The honest invariance structure.** The phase measure `ν_b = (1/n)Σ_{x∈μ_n} δ_{bx/p}` lives on
the p-point cyclic group `(1/p)Z/Z` and is **exactly invariant under multiplication by μ_n** — a
**rank-1** (cyclic) action. It is NOT ×2-invariant (2 ∉ μ_n generically), and no second
independent multiplication exists:

**The procyclic kill.** Furstenberg/Rudolph–Johnson/BLMV rigidity requires a **non-lacunary
rank-≥2 semigroup** (×a, ×b multiplicatively independent). The available actions here are (i) on
the phase space Z/p: the cyclic group μ_n; (ii) on the exponent space Z/2^μ: the multiplier group
`(Z/2^μ)^× ≅ {±1} × ⟨3⟩` — **virtually (pro)cyclic** (`Z₂^×` mod torsion is procyclic). There is
no multiplicatively independent pair; the ×2×3 hypothesis is **structurally unsatisfiable on
2-power exponent spaces**. (Side observation: for 3-smooth domain sizes `n = 2^a·3^b` the
multiplier group has rank 2 and this door opens a crack — a non-prize curiosity, since the prize
fixes dyadic n; also the b-blindness and F0 fences would still apply.)

**The rank-1 statement is the SOTA.** "A probability measure on Z/p invariant under a
multiplicative subgroup of size ≥ p^δ has small nontrivial Fourier coefficients" — the honest
measure-rigidity statement available — **is the Bourgain–Glibichuk–Konyagin theorem verbatim**
(the sup over b of `ν̂` IS `M(n,p)/n`). Direction III, instantiated rigorously, re-derives the
`n^{1−o(1)}` SOTA with its ineffective `o(1)` and nothing more.

**Rate mismatch, even fictionally.** The prize needs `|∫e dν_b| ≤ √(2 ln p/n) ≈ 2^{−11.3}`
(n = 2^30) — an *exponential-strength* discrepancy. Effective ×a×b results (BLMV, J. Mod. Dyn.
2009) deliver logarithmic-strength savings (`(log)^{−c}`-type). Even granting the (inapplicable)
theorems, the conversion loses everything: `(83)^{−c} ≫ 2^{−11.3}` for any realistic c.

**Entropy accounting (the seed's "convert entropy lower bounds").** Entropy methods lower-bound
the dimension/entropy of the invariant measure; `ν_b` has maximal entropy trivially (uniform on
n atoms). The prize is not an entropy statement — it is a Fourier-coefficient statement at a
SPECIFIC scale, below what entropy sees (entropy is magnitude-blind at the phase level — the AUP
fence in dynamical clothing). The conversion loses everything, as anticipated.
