# R313 c=3 Relation-Web Template Sampler

Date: 2026-07-09
Issue: #466 / Proximity Prize

## Summary

R313 refines the R311 signature census by normalizing every positive collision
fiber in the dangerous depth-3 `g^21 = 3` relation web.  For each fiber, the
probe translates the largest-count vector to index zero in the signed dyadic
basis, then groups the resulting sparse vector templates by signature and
collision excess.

This does not solve the prize.  It tightens the remaining finite-combinatorial
target for the depth-3 obstruction: after quotienting by cyclic shift and sign,
the c=3 web appears to be generated by a small list of template families rather
than by an opaque collision table.

## Observations

For the two high-beta dangerous primes from R307:

- `n = 64`, `p = 926510094425921`
- `n = 128`, `p = 1716841910146256242328924544641`

the normalized template sampler reports the same three signature strata as R311:

- large: `(3n - 3, 3, 1)` with delta `24n - 18`
- middle: `(6, 3, 3)` with delta `90`
- small: `(6, 3)` with delta `36`

The large stratum collapses to exactly two sign templates, each occurring `n/2`
times.  The middle stratum has exactly twelve normalized templates in both
samples, with multiplicities summing to `2n`.  The small stratum has
`6(n - 7)` normalized templates in both samples (`342` for `n = 64`, `726` for
`n = 128`), exposing what looks like six one-parameter template families with
seven excluded offsets.
