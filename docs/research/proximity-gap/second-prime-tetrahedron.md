U_{ab}(t)=(a+b)t-ab,\qquad

Allow any projective change of the quotient $T=P/Q$, including a vertex at infinity. On an $M$-node power quotient each node has $h=n/M$ physical sites. The same direction counts hold for any nondegenerate tetrahedron: the two neighboring edges in each face give a single direction from an edge-owned point, and the opposite edge supplies at most one more.
