# Four list thresholds over the order-32 grid in characteristic 277

The budget-three field of highest extension degree admitting an order-32 grid is
$\mathbb F_{277^{16}}$. On that grid the first four-word list balls occur at
**10, 16, 20 and 23 errors** in dimensions 16, 8, 4 and 2 respectively.
The operational suprema are those counts divided by 32. This also holds on
every nonzero coset of the subgroup at every positive finite interleaving width.

More generally take an odd prime $p$ and let $f$ be the least positive integer
for which $p^f=\pm1\pmod {32}$. Over any field of characteristic $p$ containing
a primitive 32nd root, the same four first-four-word counts hold provided
$p^{2f}>2^{128}$. In particular this applies to *every* budget-three field
$\mathbb F_{p^e}$ admitting the grid with $e\le 2f$: then
$p^{2f}\ge p^e\ge3\cdot2^{128}$. The ambient field can be larger than the
subfield generated by the evaluation sites.

## Why the residue degree appears twice

Let $z$ be a complex primitive 32nd root, $K=\mathbb Q(z)$ and
$E=\mathbb Q(z+z^{-1})$. Use the [length-32 comparison](mu32-list-threshold-large-characteristic.md#lifting-a-constant-ratio-of-vanishers)
for two disjoint equal-sized supports and two test sites outside the supports.
Write $U=L_A(x)L_B(y)$, $V=L_A(y)L_B(x)$, $Z=U-V$. Conjugation sends
$x-a$ to $-(x-a)/(xa)$, so the two products acquire the same factor.
Thus $Z/V=U/V-1$ belongs to $E$. All odd primes see $V$ as a unit because
each difference of distinct dyadic roots is a unit away from 2.

At any prime over $p$ in $E$ the residue degree is $f$: the Frobenius orbit
of the reduction of $z+z^{-1}$ has that length, since
$u+u^{-1}=v+v^{-1}$ exactly when $u=v$ or $u=v^{-1}$. Equivalently
Frobenius in the real cyclotomic Galois group is the class of $p$ modulo sign.
Suppose a comparison becomes an equality on reduction at the chosen primitive
root but $Z\ne0$ in $K$. In $E$ the valuation of $Z/V$ is positive at
the restricted prime and nonnegative at every prime above $p$. Therefore
the rational norm of $Z/V$ from $E$ has $p$-adic valuation at least $f$.
In $K$ the norm of this real element is its real-field norm **squared**.
Multiplying by the norm of $V$ does not change this valuation. Hence
$p^{2f}$ divides the odd part of the nonzero integer norm of $Z$.
No assumption that the primitive root lies in the prime field is needed.

For degrees 5 through 8 and nonantipodal test pairs the previously certified
upper bound for that odd part is $2^{130246/1024}<2^{128}$. Thus the
comparison lifts. The graph of nonantipodal pairs connects any three or more
test sites. The published [half-rate triangle/cycle census](mu32-list-threshold-large-characteristic.md#the-list-upper-bound-at-nine-errors)
and [quarter-rate fiber census](mu32-quarter-list-threshold.md#three-finite-fiber-facts)
apply with their original supports and centers. Their lower constructions
specialize because their denominators involve distinct grid sites. The two
lower-rate rows already hold over every field admitting the grid.
[Four-rate table, coset transformation and interleaving argument](mu32-quarter-list-threshold.md)

## Which high-degree field occurs?

Budget three means $3\cdot2^{128}\le p^e<2^{130}$. If $e\ge13$, then
$p<1024$. Characteristic two has no order-32 multiplicative subgroup. For
each odd prime below 1024 there is at most one exponent in the budget
interval, since adjacent powers differ by a factor at least three. Enumerating
these primes, testing the interval and $32\mid p^e-1$ leaves just $(277,16)$.
Its real residue degree is 8, and $277^{16}=1201368678463463241714833837971996935361$.
By contrast $1783^{12}$ also has budget three and an order-32 subgroup, but
$1783^8<2^{128}$ and its real residue degree is 4. This certificate leaves
that field open.

For a concrete realization of the grid, in characteristic 277 one has
$60^2=-1$. Every root of $X^8-60$ has order 32; the multiplicative order
of 277 modulo 32 is 8, so the polynomial is irreducible. Its roots generate
the degree-eight subfield of the ambient degree-sixteen field. The verifier
reduces the four integral degree-seven words in this representation and
evaluates a single center against all four. Each has exactly 16 agreements.

Run `python3 scripts/probes/probe_mu32_residue_list.py` for the norm bound,
high-degree field census, negative control and specialized quarter-rate
witness. This numerical instance uses 32 sites. The two designated
length-$2^{30}$ list thresholds remain open.
