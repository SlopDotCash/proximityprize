[Part 3] Is mgf_charp == mom_charp?  (does the cosh-MGF add anything over moments?)
------------------------------------------------------------
  The char-p cosh-MGF bound and the char-p moment bound should be numerically equal
  (cosh = generating fn of the SAME even moments). If mgf_p ~ mom_p, the MGF is a
  REPACKAGING of the moments, not new information.  And mgf_0 (char-0 RHS) inherits
  the SAME anomaly wall as mom_0 (the char-0 RHS is only valid below the anomaly).
