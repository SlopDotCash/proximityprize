 * The orbit-fold deflation (d+1)*foldedAgree <= plainAgree is EXACT, lossless, factor d+1=2,
   provable for ANY 2-to-1 fold over ANY field (FoldingTransferNoGo.lean) -- a pure combinatorial
   counting fact, INDEPENDENT of cancellation.

   and is fully reproduced by R_bgk = 2*(c(n)/c(n/2))^2*log(q/n)/log(2q/n) with c=M/sqrt(n log)
   STABLE -- i.e. M(n) lives on the BGK sqrt(n*log(q/n)) law.
 * The factor '2' in M^2/M(n/2)^2 is the sqrt(n)->sqrt(2n) DOUBLING of the size n inside
   sqrt(n*log), which happens for ANY size-doubling subgroup -- it is NOT produced by the
   x->x^2 ORBIT structure. (Same '2' would appear comparing mu_n to an UNRELATED mu_{n/2}.)
Conclusion: the two '2's are unrelated: one is a lossless integer orbit count, the other is a
sqrt-of-size scaling sitting ON TOP of the open BGK cancellation. The walls do NOT share a
deflation mechanism in the load-bearing sense (W-BGK's '2' is the trivial size factor; the
HARD content of W-BGK is the c(n)=M/sqrt(n log) constant, which the orbit fold says nothing about).
