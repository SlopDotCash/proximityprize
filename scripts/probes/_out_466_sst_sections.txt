THEORY NOTE (exact, not empirical): the shift S -> S+1 multiplies the defining
linear form sum c_i h^{s_i} by the UNIT h, so the section lattice is the same
subgroup of Z^{2r} up to the sorting permutation of coordinates.  Sparse counts,
lambda1, lambda1* are therefore EXACTLY constant on shift orbits.  (d) verifies.
STRUCTURAL sparse vectors: n = 2^k => h^{n/2} = -1 => every section containing an
antipodal pair {s, s+n/2} has the char-0 vector e_s + e_{s+n/2}.  For n a 2-power
ALL char-0 vanishing {0,+-1} sums of n-th roots of unity decompose into antipodal
pairs, so: bad section is CHAR-0/structural iff it contains an antipodal pair;
bad ANTIPODAL-FREE sections are the sporadic char-p defect (the K^r question).

progression of 2-powers (maximally structured/correlated instance). Kept for
contrast but statistics are read off the two generic primes 65617, 65633.
